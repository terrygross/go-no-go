Process

Three steps, one answer.

01

Set your boat once

Tell us your hull type, length, skipper experience, and home marina. GO / NO-GO uses your limits — not a generic coastal forecast.

02

Check the verdict

Live wind, gusts, wave height, and wave period are pulled for your exact location. The worst factor decides the verdict — not an average.

03

Go with confidence

See the 10-day plan, hourly breakdown, and exactly why the verdict is what it is. Then you decide — the skipper always calls it.

What's inside

🎯

Personalised limits

A 4m RIB and a 9m cabin cruiser get different answers to the same sea. Limits scale with your hull type, length, and experience.

🌊

Wave period as a safety metric

Most apps show height. We also show wave period — a 4-second chop is far rougher than a 1.5 ft height alone suggests.

📅

10-day planning

See morning and afternoon verdicts for the next 10 days. The app finds your best window automatically.

⚠ Important safety disclaimer

GO / NO-GO is a decision-support tool only. It is not a navigation system, not a certified safety service, and not a substitute for proper seamanship, local knowledge, or professional maritime advice.

Every verdict is generated automatically from third-party forecast data. No verdict constitutes permission, clearance, or a professional recommendation to launch or remain ashore.

THE SKIPPER'S JUDGEMENT IS ALWAYS FINAL AND ALWAYS SOLE.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, GO / NO-GO and its developer(s) accept no liability whatsoever for any injury, death, loss, or damage arising from decisions made using this application; inaccurate, delayed, or missing forecast data; or conditions that differ from those forecast. You use this application entirely at your own risk.

🔒 Your boat profile never leaves your phone

Your boat profile is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to any server. Your account holds your name, email and sign-in, nothing more. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. Full details in the privacy policy.

📡 Data sources

Forecast data from Open-Meteo and Copernicus Marine Service. Provided "as received" — GO / NO-GO has no control over their accuracy or availability.
