02 What GO / NO-GO is, and what it is not

GO / NO-GO turns marine weather forecasts into a simple verdict (GO, THINK TWICE or NO-GO) for recreational powerboats, using the boat, crew and trip details you enter. It is a decision-support tool only. It is not a navigation system, not a certified safety or rescue service, and not a substitute for seamanship, local knowledge, official forecasts or professional advice.

A verdict is produced automatically from forecast data. It is never permission, clearance or a professional recommendation to go out or to stay ashore. Conditions guidance only. The final call is always the skipper’s.

03 Weather and sea data: we try our best, but we cannot promise accuracy

We work hard to present the most accurate guidance we can. But we do not create weather data. Forecasts come from independent third-party providers, including Open-Meteo and the Copernicus Marine Service, and we use them as received. We cannot control their accuracy, resolution, timeliness or availability.

Marine forecasts are predictions, not facts. Conditions can differ from what is forecast, can change faster than any model can predict, and can vary sharply from one headland, bay or hour to the next. Our thresholds are general guides for each boat class and cannot account for your exact boat, loading, experience, crew, equipment or local effects. Data can be late, missing or wrong, and the app can be unavailable. You must always check the sea and sky yourself, and consult official sources (for example your national weather service and navigational warnings) before leaving shore.
